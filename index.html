About

Since July 2026, Wilfredo has been a contract Systems Engineer at Hyper Nimbus, an AI company. He was brought in for infrastructure, security, CI/CD and identity on a government digital-services platform. His role soon grew to cover several products on shared AWS infrastructure: an AI assistant for a consumer e-commerce brand, a lead-generation platform for financial-services agents, and Hyper Bricks, an AI workspace that launched publicly in October 2026.

His work runs from single sign-on and least-privilege access to production troubleshooting, security reviews, payments integration and compliance policy. He works closely with a distributed engineering team, and much of his day-to-day work is done alongside AI coding agents. He believes in checking the real state of a system before changing it, and in giving everyone, himself included, only the access they need.

Working principles

  1. Check the real state of a system before acting on it.
  2. Give everyone, me included, only the access they need.
  3. Treat any credential that has been exposed as compromised, and replace it.
  4. Prefer reversible, reviewable changes over direct edits to shared systems.
  5. Correct my own mistakes openly, where the original was posted.
